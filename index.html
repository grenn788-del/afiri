Pink October: standing together for early detection

October 2026

Every October, the world unites to raise awareness about breast cancer. Afiri Consulting International joins this movement through its commitment to women’s health, dignity, and empowerment.

  1. 01

    Raise awareness

    Sharing clear, trustworthy information about breast health and the importance of regular check-ups.

  2. 02

    Encourage early detection

    Early screening saves lives. We encourage everyone to know their options and speak with a health professional.

  3. 03

    Support our communities

    Standing with women, survivors, and families through solidarity, listening, and access to resources.

About Afiri

Strategy rooted in lived experience

Afiri Consulting International is a growing consulting organization supported by professionals with real-world, sector-specific experience. We provide strategic guidance and actionable solutions to organizations, institutions, and individuals working in human rights, education, leadership, humanitarian action, advocacy, and community development.

Integrity

Sustainable impact
