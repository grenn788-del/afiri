Privacy Policy

Last updated : October 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Afiri Consulting International collects through this website, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short on purpose: this website collects very little.

Minimal data

Only what you choose to send us through the contact form, plus basic technical logs.

No tracking

No advertising cookies, no analytics, no profiling and no sale of data.

Your rights

Access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to us.

Who we are

Afiri Consulting International (“Afiri”, “we”, “us”) operates this website and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at contact@afiriconsulting.com or by phone at +1 202 297 2212.

Data we collect

We collect only the following:

  • Contact form: your full name, email address, organization (optional), area of interest, your message, your consent, and the language of the page you used.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting server automatically records the information needed to deliver and secure the site (IP address, browser type, pages requested, date and time).
  • Direct messages: anything you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Please do not include sensitive information (health data, identity numbers, payment details) in your message. We do not need it to answer you.

What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising cookies, analytics or profiling tools on this website.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.
  • We do not add you to a newsletter without a separate, explicit request.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

How we use your data

  • To read and answer your inquiry and propose a suitable next step.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.
  • To protect the website against spam and abuse (for example through a hidden anti-spam field and a security token on the form).
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Legal basis

We process the data you submit through the form on the basis of your consent (the box you tick before sending). We process technical data and business correspondence on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and securing the site and answering professional requests, and where required, to comply with the law.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to contact@afiriconsulting.com. This does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not set tracking cookies. The only cookie that may appear is a language cookie (googtrans), created when you choose a language that is machine-translated (any language other than English and French). It only remembers your language choice and is removed when you return to English or French.

The English and French versions of the site are written natively and load no Google code.

Third-party services and links

  • Google Translate: loaded only if you choose a machine-translated language. The text of the page is then sent to Google for translation, under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Hosting and email providers: service providers that process data on our behalf and on our instructions.
  • External links: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, WhatsApp and the GreenLab Design portfolio are operated by third parties with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for them.

Sharing and international transfers

We share personal data only with the service providers described above and with public authorities when the law requires it. Because we work worldwide and our providers may be located in other countries, your data may be processed outside your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected.

How long we keep your data

We keep your inquiry for as long as needed to handle it and any resulting relationship, then for a reasonable period for record-keeping, generally no longer than three years after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Technical logs are kept for a short period only.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), a security token and an anti-spam field on the form, and we limit access to your data to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if a problem occurs.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, or its use restricted;
  • object to certain processing, and receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise a right, write to contact@afiriconsulting.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Write to contact@afiriconsulting.com or call +1 202 297 2212.
